Renvi Economia

Renvi Economia is a Windows desktop video editor used by the Renvi Economia channel. It cuts long videos into short vertical clips and sends them to the creator's own accounts (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok). On TikTok, clips are sent to the creator's inbox as drafts, and the creator publishes them in the TikTok app.
